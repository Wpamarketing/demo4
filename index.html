The Girl Behind the Rockets

Hi! I'm Manisha Roy, a passionate Aerospace Engineer with a love for pushing boundaries beyond Earth's atmosphere. My journey started with a childhood fascination with the stars and evolved into a career designing cutting-edge aerospace systems.

I specialize in propulsion systems, aerodynamics, and spacecraft design. When I'm not running simulations or drafting schematics, you'll find me stargazing or sketching my next big idea.
